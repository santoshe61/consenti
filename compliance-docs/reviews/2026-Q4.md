- **Jurisdiction / regulation:** UK-GDPR / PECR
- **Source:** `ico.org.uk/for-organisations/guide-to-pecr/cookies-and-similar-technologies/` — retrieved 2026-10-08 — page "Last updated 29 April 2026". Already in force (exceptions took effect February 2026 per Q3 sources and the ICO's own framing of them as new).

- **Impact classification:** Uncertain — needs human legal review (low-risk docs-only path available)

### F6 (new) — California SB 923 (Expanding Privacy Rights Act): deletion right regardless of source

- **Jurisdiction / regulation:** CCPA / CPRA
- **Source:** `privacy.ca.gov/2026/09/california-expands-privacy-protections-by-strengthening-deletion-rights/` — retrieved 2026-10-08 — announced 2026-09-27; **effective 2027-01-01** (announced, not yet in force).
- **What changed:** per the CPPA's newsroom article, SB 923 requires covered businesses to delete personal information on a deletion request regardless of its source (closing a loophole that let businesses keep data obtained from third parties) and mandates online submission methods for privacy requests (the webform provision applies to online-only businesses). - **Impact classification:** No product impact (Consenti captures and stores consent; it is not a DSAR/deletion system. Its erasure support — `widget.deleteConsent()` and the modal's "Forget me" control — concerns consent records and is unaffected).
- **Suggested change (draft only — not applied):**
  - Code: N/A.
  - Docs: optional one-line mention on `apps/docs/src/app/docs/compliance/ccpa/page.tsx` / `cpra/page.tsx` where the right to delete is described, noting the 2027-01-01 change; not worth a standalone PR.
  - Behavior-change risk: None.
