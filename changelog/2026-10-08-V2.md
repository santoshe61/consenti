## [0.3.1] - 2026-10-08
### Summary
On the MySQL/MariaDB driver, the dashboard timeline received dates like `"Thu Oct 08 2026 00:00:00 GMT+0530 (India Standard Time)"` instead of `YYYY-MM-DD`, because mysql2 returns `DATE()` results as JS `Date` objects and the adapter stringified them. Dates are now formatted in SQL, matching the PostgreSQL adapter.
